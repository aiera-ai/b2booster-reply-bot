Arhitektura

This is not a single tool. It is a secure AI layer over the systems ACTUAL I.T. already uses.

Nothing gets replaced. The AI reads from your existing sources, processes information, and delivers results where your team already works.

Your CRM, document storage, email, and project management tools stay exactly as they are. The AI layer connects to them via APIs and reads what it needs - project records, proposal templates, client communication history.

Processing happens on secure infrastructure. Every query is logged, every output is traceable. Your data does not leave controlled environments and is never used to train third-party models.

Each module works independently. You can start with proposal drafting alone and add pipeline visibility or reporting later. Nothing depends on everything else being live first.
