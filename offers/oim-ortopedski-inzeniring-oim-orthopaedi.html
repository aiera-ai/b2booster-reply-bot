Context

In orthopaedic device manufacturing, the bottleneck is rarely the engineering - it is the documentation, traceability, and regulatory overhead that surrounds every product iteration

OIM designs and manufactures custom orthopaedic implants and instruments. Every new variant triggers a cascade of technical documentation - design history files, risk analyses, biocompatibility rationale, labelling updates. The engineering team spends significant hours on paperwork that follows strict but repetitive patterns. That is time taken directly from development and market entry.

Possible solutions

What we could build for OIM Orthopaedic Engineering

Six modules mapped to the processes your team runs daily - not generic AI features.

✦ 01

AI for technical file assembly

Pulls relevant sections from existing DHFs, risk analyses, and test reports to draft new technical documentation shells. Reduces copy-paste cycles and flags missing sections before review.

Primer uporabe
"Draft technical file for tibial component v2.4 is ready - 3 sections need your input, 14 were pre-filled from v2.3."
◎ 02

Regulatory cross-reference engine

Maps your product claims against applicable standards - ISO 13485, ISO 14971, MDR Annex II/III - and highlights gaps. Updates when standards change so you know before an audit does.

Primer uporabe
"ISO 14971:2019 clause 10.2 is referenced in 4 of your risk files but the referenced version is 2012. Review recommended."
⊙ 03

AI-assisted CAPA management

Ingests complaint data, production nonconformities, and field feedback. Groups related events, suggests root cause categories, and drafts initial CAPA descriptions for quality review.

Primer uporabe
"3 complaints in Q1 relate to surface finish on femoral component lot 2024-F09. Suggested CAPA category: manufacturing process control."
⌬ 04

Internal knowledge assistant for QMS

Your quality management system holds hundreds of SOPs, work instructions, and forms. The assistant answers natural-language questions from any team member and returns the exact document section.

Primer uporabe
"Sterilisation validation protocol for Class III devices is in SOP-QA-042, section 5.3. Last revision: March 2024."
⎈ 05

Supplier audit and qualification tracker

Consolidates approved supplier lists, audit schedules, and certificate expiry dates into a single dashboard. Sends alerts before a qualification lapses so procurement acts in time.

Primer uporabe
"Titanium rod supplier audit certificate expires in 22 days. Last audit score: 87/100. Re-audit scheduling suggested."
▣ 06

Post-market surveillance reporting

Aggregates complaint trends, vigilance data, and literature monitoring into periodic PMS reports. Pre-fills PSUR and trend analysis sections so the RA team reviews rather than writes from scratch.

Primer uporabe
"Q1 PSUR draft for hip stem product family is ready. 2 trend signals flagged for clinical review."
Arhitektura

This is not a single tool. It is a secure AI layer that sits on top of your existing systems and processes.

OIM keeps working with its current QMS, ERP, and file storage. The AI layer connects to them - it does not replace them.

Every module reads from your existing data sources - QMS documents, ERP records, shared drives, email inboxes. No migration required. The layer indexes what is already there and makes it queryable, cross-referenceable, and actionable.

Access control mirrors your current permission structure. An engineer sees engineering data. A quality manager sees CAPA records. Nothing leaks sideways. Every AI action is logged with a timestamp and user ID.

The system is modular. Start with one use case - say, technical file assembly. Add CAPA management later if it proves useful. Remove what does not work. No vendor lock-in, no multi-year contracts.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the realistic ROI timeline for a company our size?
The pilot is designed to show measurable time savings within 3 weeks. Typical results for documentation-heavy companies: 40-60% reduction in time spent assembling technical files. You will have concrete numbers from your own data before any broader commitment.
How much of my team's time does the pilot require?
Roughly 3-4 hours total across the pilot period - mostly in the first days for access setup and document structure walkthrough. We do the build work. Your team tests the output and gives feedback.
Will this disrupt our current QMS or validated processes?
No. The AI layer reads from your systems but does not write back to them unless explicitly configured. Your validated processes stay untouched. Think of it as a smart assistant sitting beside your team, not inside your QMS.
How do you handle confidential product data and IP?
We sign an NDA and DPA before seeing any data. All processing runs on EU-hosted infrastructure. Your data is never used to train third-party models. Access is restricted to named project members only.
What happens if the pilot does not deliver what we expect?
You stop. There is no ongoing contract tied to the pilot. If the results are not useful for your team, we document what we learned and part ways. The deliverables from the pilot remain yours regardless.
Can this scale to cover multiple product families and markets?
Yes, but deliberately. After the pilot proves value on one product family, we extend to others one at a time. Each extension is scoped separately. You control the pace and the budget.
